1 Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how EduTech Solutions LLC uses "cookies" and similar technologies on its website and applications (hereinafter, the "Site"). The objective is to provide you with clear information about what data we collect, for what purpose, and how you can manage them.

2 What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the Site to remember your actions and preferences (for example, language or login) for a certain period of time, to improve your user experience.

3 Types of cookies we use

Allow us to show you relevant ads and measure campaign effectiveness.

5 How to manage or disable cookies

You have several options to control cookies:

1. From your browser

Chrome: Menu ⋮ → Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
Firefox: Menu ☰ → Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and site data.
Edge: Menu ⋮ → Settings → Cookies and site permissions.
Safari (macOS): Preferences → Privacy → Manage website data.

2. Cookie banner

When entering the Site, use the banner options to accept or reject cookie categories.

3. External tools

  • Services like YourOnlineChoices or Network Advertising Initiative to manage advertising cookies.

6 Consent and changes

By continuing to browse our Site without changing your browser settings or clicking "Reject cookies" on our banner, you accept the use of cookies in accordance with this Policy.

We may update this Policy at any time. The "Last updated" date indicates its validity. We recommend reviewing it periodically.
