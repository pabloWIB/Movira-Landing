Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 June 2025

This Privacy Policy sets out the terms under which MOVIRA uses and protects the information provided by its users when they use its website. This company is committed to the security of its users' data. When we ask you to fill in fields with personal information by which you can be identified, we do so ensuring that it will only be used in accordance with the terms of this document. However, this Privacy Policy may change over time or be updated, so we recommend that you review this page regularly to make sure you agree with those changes.

Information we collect

Our website may collect personal information, for example: name, contact details such as your email address, and demographic information. Likewise, when necessary, specific information may be requested in order to process an order or carry out a delivery or invoicing.

Use of the information collected

Our website uses the information in order to provide the best possible service, particularly to keep a record of users, of orders where applicable, and to improve our products and services. Emails may be sent periodically through our site with special offers, new products and other promotional information we consider relevant to you or that may be of benefit to you. These emails will be sent to the address you provide and may be cancelled at any time.

MOVIRA is strongly committed to keeping your information secure. We use the most advanced systems and update them constantly to make sure there is no unauthorised access.

Cookies

A cookie is a file that is sent in order to request permission to be stored on your computer. Once you accept the file it is created, and the cookie then serves to hold information about web traffic, and also makes future visits to a recurring site easier. Another function cookies have is that with them websites can recognise you individually and therefore offer you the best personalised service on their site.

Our website uses cookies to identify which pages are visited and how often. This information is used solely for statistical analysis and the information is then permanently deleted. You can delete cookies from your computer at any time. However, cookies help websites provide a better service; they do not give access to information about your computer or about you, unless you choose to provide it directly. You can accept or decline the use of cookies; however, most browsers accept cookies automatically, as this makes for a better web service. You can also change your computer settings to decline cookies. If they are declined, you may not be able to use some of our services.

Links to third parties

This website may contain links to other sites that could be of interest to you. Once you click on these links and leave our page, we no longer have control over the site you are redirected to and are therefore not responsible for the terms or privacy, nor for the protection of your data, on those third-party sites. Those sites are subject to their own privacy policies, so it is advisable that you consult them to confirm that you agree with them.

Control of your personal information

At any time you may restrict the collection or use of the personal information provided to our website. Each time you are asked to fill in a form, such as a user registration form, you can tick or untick the option to receive information by email. If you have ticked the option to receive our newsletter or advertising, you may cancel it at any time.

This company will not sell, transfer or distribute the personal information collected without your consent, unless required by a judge with a court order.

MOVIRA reserves the right to change the terms of this Privacy Policy at any time.

Contact

For any question about this policy or about the processing of your data, write to us at contacto@movira.com.
