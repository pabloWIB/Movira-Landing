Who We Are

Revolutionizing B2B Distribution in Colombia

MOVIRA was born from the vision of Pablo Nieto Pérez and Juan Sebastián García Ramírez to transform business logistics. We create intelligent connections between companies and external distributors, optimizing the supply chain without infrastructure investments.

Our Mission

Provide companies with flexible operational capacity without liabilities or fixed costs, through the integration of advanced technology and logistics tools that optimize every distribution process with reliable external distributors.

Our Vision

To be the leading B2B platform in Latin America that connects companies with external distribution networks, establishing the standard for logistics efficiency and digital transformation in the distribution sector.

Frequently Asked Questions

We Answer Your Questions

Find answers about our B2B distribution platform

How does the MOVIRA platform work?

MOVIRA connects your company with a network of verified external distributors. Our technology integrates these distributors into your logistics processes, optimizing routes and delivery times without requiring investment in infrastructure or hiring additional staff.

What advantages does working with external distributors offer?

By working with external distributors through MOVIRA, you reduce fixed costs, expand your geographical coverage, improve delivery times and obtain flexible operational capacity that adapts to demand without compromising capital in assets or personnel.

How does MOVIRA integrate with our current processes?

Our platform integrates easily with your existing systems through APIs and standard connectors. The implementation process is gradual and our team accompanies you in each phase to ensure a transition without interruptions in your operations.

How do you guarantee the quality of distributors?

All our distributors go through a rigorous verification process that includes document validation, operational capacity, commercial references and continuous performance evaluation. We only work with distributors that maintain our quality standards.

What type of companies can benefit from MOVIRA?

MOVIRA is ideal for companies of all sizes looking to expand their distribution reach without infrastructure investment: e-commerce, manufacturing, retail, startups and any business that requires efficient and scalable deliveries.
