How it works

From the first conversation to a confirmed delivery

Nothing has to be migrated. MOVIRA plugs into the operation you already run and starts with the surplus that costs you most today.

  1. 1

    You write to us

    We book a meeting and work out together which part of your operation to support first. You can also start through a pilot programme.

  2. 2

    We connect systems

    Send orders through the API or manage them from the dashboard. No need to change your ERP or rebuild your internal processes.

  3. 3

    The network takes the order

    Surplus orders and hard-to-cover areas go to verified independent drivers, with capacity that adjusts to your demand.

  4. 4

    You track and confirm

    Follow the route live until the end customer confirms. Once the delivery is verified, the driver is paid automatically.

Solutions

What the platform includes

Four pieces that solve the same problem: move more orders without growing your fixed structure.

Verified driver network

Every driver goes through identity validation, document checks and basic delivery training before taking their first order.

Live tracking

A dashboard with the route of every order from pickup to delivery. Each movement is traced and subject to confirmation by the end customer.

API integration

Connect your current systems to the platform without friction. Orders come in and their status flows back with no manual steps.

Automatic payments

When your company verifies the delivery, the driver is paid without anyone stepping in. MOVIRA charges a commission agreed in advance.

Why MOVIRA

Capacity when you need it, without putting it on the balance sheet

Complements, does not replace

Your operation stays yours. MOVIRA steps in where capacity runs short: surplus orders and difficult areas.

Adjusts to demand

Scale up in peak season and back down when it passes, with no idle vehicles and no staff on payroll waiting for orders.

Traceable end to end

Authentication, data encryption and real-time validation. Every delivery is logged and confirmed by the end customer.

MOVIRA came out of the work of Pablo Nieto Pérez and Juan Sebastián García Ramírez on a concrete problem in Colombian logistics: growing your coverage forces you to buy assets that later sit idle.

The alternative is an external network of independent drivers already on the road, verified and coordinated from a single platform. We currently operate in Colombia and are ready to scale to other regions depending on demand and partnerships.
