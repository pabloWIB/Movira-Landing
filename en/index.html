Frequently Asked Questions

We Resolve Your Doubts

Find answers to the most common questions about our services

How does the Academic Management System (Tecdu) work?

Our SGA - Tecdu system centralizes the administration of grades, attendance and enrollment in a single platform. It is accessible from any internet-connected device and easily integrates with other educational management tools, complying with all CACES standards in Ecuador.

What advantages does server rental with 24/7 support offer?

By renting cloud servers with our 24/7 support, you can scale resources according to your needs, without worrying about physical hardware maintenance. Our team handles continuous monitoring, updates and security so you can focus on growing your project.

How does online payment integration work?

Once the legal structure (e.g., LLC) and bank account are set up, we work with payment providers like Stripe, PayPal, etc., to securely process transactions and accept multiple payment methods. We ensure compliance with standards like PCI DSS to protect user data.

Do you offer international support?

Yes, we provide services and technical assistance to institutions and companies in different countries. We adapt our plans to each region, considering aspects of language, schedule and local regulations. Our team is trained to work in different time zones and comply with international regulations.

How can I access support after completing a project?

We offer post-launch maintenance and support plans that include updates, performance monitoring and incident resolution. You can contact our team at any time to ensure the continuity of your technological solution. We have different support levels according to your needs.
